PickleWatch: pickleball tracker for Apple Watch

Your Apple Watch names and grades every swing while you play: dinks, drives, volleys, slices and smashes. No phone on the court, no extra sensors, no setup.

Free on Apple Watch · Rated 4.6 on the App Store

What PickleWatch tracks

  • Every shot, named

    Picks out dinks, drives, volleys, slices and smashes from your wrist motion. Dinks, drives and volleys are split into forehand and backhand.

  • Instant grades

    Your watch flashes green, yellow or red after each swing, so you see your form grade right away.

  • Estimated swing speed

    See an estimated speed for each swing in mph, plus your top speed and how consistent you are.

  • Pickleball score keeper

    Tap to add points for you or your opponents. It plays to 11, win by 2, and flags game point.

  • Heart rate and calories

    Sessions save to Apple Health as pickleball workouts, if you allow it.

  • Progress on iPhone

    Session summaries, trends, streaks and achievements show where you're improving.

How it works

  1. Open PickleWatch on your Apple Watch and tap start.
  2. Play your game. Leave your phone in your bag.
  3. See every shot, your grades and your speed when you finish.

Questions

Is there a pickleball app for Apple Watch?

Yes. PickleWatch runs on your Apple Watch and tracks every pickleball swing automatically. It also keeps score and can save each session as a pickleball workout.

Is PickleWatch free?

Yes, it's free to download and free on Apple Watch. An optional PickleWatch Pro subscription on iPhone unlocks deeper shot analysis and longer history charts.

Do I need my iPhone on the pickleball court?

No. Your watch tracks every rally on its own. Sessions move to the iPhone app once your phone is back in range.

Which devices does it work on?

An Apple Watch with watchOS 9.6 or later, paired with an iPhone on iOS 17.6 or later.

Does it work if I wear my watch on my left wrist?

Yes. Choose your wrist on the watch before you play so forehands and backhands are read correctly.

How does PickleWatch estimate swing speed?

It works out how fast your paddle travels from the rotation of your wrist, read by the watch's motion sensors. Treat it as an estimate, not a radar reading of ball speed.

Where does my data go?

The privacy policy says your swing stats stay on your devices. The app sends anonymous usage analytics (Google Analytics and Smartlook) to help improve it. Read the PickleWatch privacy policy.
