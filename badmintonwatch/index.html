BadmintonWatch: badminton tracker for Apple Watch

Wear your watch on your racket hand and play. BadmintonWatch counts your swings, rates each one and keeps the rally score to 21. Afterwards it sorts your shots into clears, drops, smashes, drives, net shots, lifts and blocks.

Free on Apple Watch · iPhone app needs a subscription

BadmintonWatch in short

What it is
An Apple Watch app that counts badminton swings and keeps the rally score. On the App Store it's listed as "BadmintonWatch: Badminton".
Who it's for
Badminton players who wear an Apple Watch on their racket hand and want swing counts, shot types and the score without a phone on court.
What it tracks
A swing count, with each swing rated green, yellow or red. Clears, drops, smashes, drives, net shots, lifts, blocks and serves. Estimated racket speed in mph, rally score to 21, heart rate and calories.
What it costs
Free to download, and swing tracking and scoring on the Apple Watch are free. The iPhone app, with your past games and trends, needs a subscription.
What you need
An Apple Watch on watchOS 9.6 or later, paired with an iPhone on iOS 17.6 or later.

What your watch picks up on court

  • Badminton shots by name

    Clears, drops, smashes, drives, net shots, lifts and blocks, plus serves. Your watch's motion sensors pick them up, and the summary shows how many of each you hit.

  • Good, medium or poor

    After each swing the screen flashes green, yellow or red. When you end the session, your watch shows how your swings split between them.

  • Rally scoring to 21

    Win by 2, capped at 30, best of three. Tap a score to give that side the point, and use the minus button if you slip. A badge shows who's serving and from which court.

  • Estimated racket speed

    Your last swing in mph as you play, then your top and average speed for the session. The watch estimates it from how fast your wrist turns.

  • Apple Health workouts

    With your permission, each session is saved to Apple Health as a Badminton workout with heart rate and calories.

  • Results on your iPhone

    Look back at past games, see your trends over 7 days, 30 days or all time, and share a game card. The iPhone app needs a subscription.

How it works

  1. Open BadmintonWatch on your Apple Watch, pick the wrist you wear it on and tap START.
  2. Play. Between rallies, swipe to the score page and tap a score to give that side the point.
  3. When you finish, your watch shows your shot mix, swing ratings and estimated speed. The session then shows up in the iPhone app.

Questions

Can an Apple Watch track badminton?

Yes. Apple's built-in Badminton workout records your time, heart rate and calories. BadmintonWatch adds what it leaves out: a swing count, shot types, a rating for each swing and the rally score.

Does it use rally scoring to 21?

Yes. Every rally scores a point. Games go to 21, won by 2, and at 29-29 the next point wins. Matches are best of three. There's no option for other scoring formats.

How much does BadmintonWatch cost?

Nothing to download. Swing tracking and scoring on the watch are free. On iPhone you need a subscription to see your games, trends and full breakdown.

Can I leave my phone off court?

Yes. The watch tracks swings and score on its own. The session copies across to your iPhone once the two reconnect.

Does it work with my Apple Watch?

It does if the watch runs watchOS 9.6 or later. The BadmintonWatch app on your iPhone asks for iOS 17.6 or later.

How accurate is the speed number?

Treat it as a guide. BadmintonWatch estimates how fast your racket moves from how fast your wrist turns, adjusted for your height. Shuttle speed isn't measured.

What happens to my data?

The privacy policy says shot tracking runs on your Apple Watch and iPhone, and your detailed shot data stays on your devices. When you finish a game, a short summary with the score, game length, shot counts and top speed goes to Google Analytics. The policy also lists Firebase Crashlytics, Smartlook and RevenueCat. Workouts reach Apple Health only with your permission. The full details are in the BadmintonWatch privacy policy.
